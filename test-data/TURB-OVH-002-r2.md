# Steam Turbine Overhaul & Refurbishment Guide
**Revision**: 2.1
**Effective Date**: March 2022
**Applies To**: Siemens SST-600 Steam Turbines
**Document ID**: TURB-OVH-002-r2

## 1. Purpose

This guide defines the standard procedures for a major steam turbine overhaul, including scope definition, disassembly sequence, inspection criteria, and reassembly tolerances.

## 2. Scope

**Covered Components**:
- Rotor assembly with blading
- Casing with bearing housings
- Radial and thrust bearings
- Governing system and valves
- Oil lubrication system
- Condenser interface
- Balance shaft assembly

**Machinery Data**:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Rated Output | 25 MW |
| Steam Inlet | 1450 psig / 1000°F |
| Exhaust | 3 in Hg absolute |
| Speed | 5800 RPM (18,560 ft/min tip) |
| Rotor Mass | 4,800 lb |
| Critical Speed | 2800 RPM |

## 3. Overhaul Planning

### 3.1 Overhaul Intervals

| Overhaul Type | Interval | Duration |
|---------------|----------|----------|
| Minor (Borescope & seats) | 12 months | 2 days |
| Standard (Major) | 36 months | 14 days |
| Extended Major | 84 months | 28 days |

**Planning Checklist**:
1. Review maintenance history
2. Obtain OEM tooling and fixtures
3. Procure gaskets, seals, O-rings
4. Arrange specialist technicians
5. Plan logistics (crane, rigging)
6. Verify spare parts for bearings
7. Confirm alignment equipment availability

### 3.2 Pre-Shutdown Inspections

1. Record vibration baseline readings
2. Capture load vs. speed performance curve
3. Analyze oil sample for wear metals
4. Borescope inspection of hot path parts
5. Record casing/rotor clearances (cold)

## 4. Disassembly Sequence

⚠️ **HOT PARTS**: Turboanneal protocol required. Rotor cooling must not cause thermal stress. Allow minimum 48 hours natural cooling after shutdown.

### Step 1: System Isolation
1. Close main steam trip valve
2. Open drain valves
3. Disconnect oil system from turbine
4. Blind flange the casing connections
5. Vent to atmosphere

### Step 2: Upper Casing Removal
1. Remove insulation (do not discard — reusable)
2. Remove casing bolts (in diagonal sequence)
3. Install lifting eyes and lift with crane
4. Support upper casing on cribbing
5. Remove lower half after exposing internals

### Step 3: Rotor Extraction
1. Install rotor bearing jig
2. Attach rotor slings (4-point lifting arrangement)
3. Use hydraulic jacks at bearing supports
4. Lift in 1-inch increments checking balance
5. Place rotor in purpose-built cradle
6. Protect blades with acrylic spray coating

### Step 4: Bearing Removal
1. Radial bearings: unbolt and lift carrier
2. Thrust bearings: remove tilt pad assemblies
3. Record bearing temperature distribution
4. Label pads with orientation marking

## 5. Inspection Criteria

### 5.1 Rotor Blading Inspection

**Acceptance Criteria**:

| Defect | Max Allowable | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Erosion (leading edge) | 10% chord | Refurbish / blend |
| Pitting | <3/4 gap | Monitor |
| Cracking | Zero tolerance | Replace blade |
| Fretting (root) | Minor polish | Surface treatment |
| Coating loss | 40% area | Recoat |

### 5.2 Journal Bearing Criteria

- Minimum oil film: Not applicable (hydrodynamic)
- Babbit layer thickness: 0.035" min
- Clearance (new): 0.004-0.006"
- Clearance (limit): 0.010"
- Radial groove depth: inspect to spec

### 5.3 Thrust Bearing Criteria

**Tilt pad integrity**:
- Pad thickness: 1.25" nominal
- Loading surface: 230 PSI maximum
- Expected wear: 0.002" per year
- Maximum hot clearance: 0.020"

### 5.4 Casing Inspection

1. Casing flange deflection checks
2. Bolt hole thread inspection
3. Weld integrity ultrasonic testing
4. Surface cracking dye penetrant testing
5. Steam chest seat condition

## 6. Reassembly Tolerances

**Bearing Clearances** (cold, measured with plastigage):

| Location | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Radial journal bearing | 0.005" ± 0.001" |
| Thrust bearing float | 0.008" – 0.012" |
| Axial clearance (coupling end) | 0.004" – 0.008" |

**Hot Alignment**:
- Cold alignment targets must compensate for thermal growth:
  - Turbine shaft elevation: +0.150" cold to hot
  - Generator coupling gap: 0.200" cold, closes to 0.020" hot

## 7. Commissioning Checks

1. Casing to rotor clearances verified at 6 positions
2. Rotor axial float verified within spec
3. Bearing sags confirmed repeatable
4. Barring gear rotation (2 hours) before steam roll
5. Slow roll to 500 RPM for 10 minutes
6. First-stage metal temperature monitored during accelerated warm-up
7. Full-speed no-load run for 30 minutes
8. Overspeed trip tested at 110% rated speed

**Acceptance**:
- Vibration on all bearings < 2.0 mils
- Bearing temperature < 95°C
- No abnormal rubs detected
- Governing control hold ±0.25% speed

## 8. Documentation Requirements

- Overhaul inspection report with photographs
- Dimensional measurement records
- Bearing clearance survey
- Rotor runout verification
- Thermal alignment records
- Fouling/erosion history comparison
- Future maintenance recommendations
